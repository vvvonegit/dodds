Approach

An owner’s perspective

Before real estate I ran my own businesses — a courier company, and a loss adjusting firm built with my wife. That is a different education to a sales course. I know what it costs to make a payroll, and what it feels like to sign away something you built.

So a transaction is never “just business” to me. A property sale is usually the largest financial decision a family makes, and often the most emotional one. My job is to guide it personally, from the first walk of the boundary to the day of transfer — and to tell you the truth about price even when the truth is unwelcome.

You will deal with me. Not an assistant, not a call centre, not a junior who has never seen the property. I answer my own phone, I do my own viewings, and I would rather lose a mandate than win it on a number I cannot defend.

What I offer sellers is a transparent process and an honest valuation. What I offer buyers is someone who will tell them when a farm is wrong for them.
